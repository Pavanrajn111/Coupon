Escrow Workflow

The 7-step protected trade journey.

Designed to reduce fraud anxiety on both sides: the seller knows payment exists, and the buyer knows the coupon is not blindly released.

  1. 1 Seller submits coupon

    Code, brand, type, expiry, terms, and expected value are captured.

  2. 2 CouponEx verifies

    Basic validation, duplicate checks, and eligibility review happen before listing.

  3. 3 Listing goes live

    Verified metadata, discount, seller status, and risk labels are shown.

  4. 4 Buyer pays into escrow

    Funds are secured while the marketplace prepares coupon delivery.

  5. 5 Coupon is revealed

    The buyer receives the code under clear usage and confirmation rules.

  6. 6 Redemption is confirmed

    Buyer confirmation or dispute review determines the next step.

  7. 7 Funds are released

    The seller is paid and both parties update the trust record.

FAQ

Clear answers for first-time traders.

What is CouponEx?

CouponEx is a peer-to-peer digital coupon exchange marketplace where users can sell unused coupons and buyers can purchase verified deals.

How does escrow protect users?

Buyer payment is held during the transaction. Funds are released to the seller only after coupon delivery and successful confirmation or review.

Can restricted coupons be listed?

Yes, but restrictions must be visible. Region, account, category, or expiry conditions are highlighted so buyers understand usage limits before purchase.
